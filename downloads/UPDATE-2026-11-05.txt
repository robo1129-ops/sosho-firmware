SO-SHO update 2026-11-05

Left Bootloader / 左側ブートローダー:
Assign BOOTLOADER_LEFT in the web editor, save, then press the assigned key.
The right Central sends the standard bootload behavior to left peripheral 0.
The left module must be powered on and connected over the split link.
The right module stays in normal mode. USB must be connected to the left
module to access its UF2 drive after the action.
既存の左側ファームに標準bootloadが含まれる場合、そのまま利用できます。
左側が起動モードになった後、UF2を書き込むには左側へのUSB接続が必要です。

Trackball reporting / トラックボール報告周期:
USB powered: 12 ms (about 83 reports/s).
Battery powered: 18 ms (about 56 reports/s, two thirds of powered rate).
CPI, movement scale and orientation remain unchanged.
Pending movement is accumulated and sent by delayed work, including the
last movement when the ball stops between reporting slots.

Physical bootloader entry and battery-powered smoothness require hardware
verification after flashing; build success alone does not prove these.
